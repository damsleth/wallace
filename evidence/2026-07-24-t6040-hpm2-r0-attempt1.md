## Root cause

The original `19a5c2be` full-tree CRC belongs to the captured firmware ADT.
The sanctioned `chainload.py -r` path necessarily rewrites volatile
`/chosen/memory-map` handoff addresses (SEPFW, BootArgs, and preoslog when
present) and serializes the updated ADT before the candidate starts. The
safety-relevant J614s, chip, board, controller, endpoint, SID, class, and port
properties are unchanged, but the whole-tree CRC is not stable across this
standard handoff.

This was a useful fail-closed result, not an SPMI result. Ticket 093 remains
open and R1/R2 remain blocked.
