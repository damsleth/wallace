## Status and caveats

- The NVMe daily root on the 170 GiB `linux` partition is **working** at
  maxcpus=1: desktop, WiFi, BT service, keyboard, trackpad firmware and
  Norwegian layout, with the root on the internal SSD.
- ANS2 has no Flush, so a crash or hard reset loses the last writes; only a
  clean `reboot`/`poweroff` commits them. `poweroff` has the same
  device_shutdown path, but the final power-off is untested: `smc_reboot` and
  the PMU are disabled, so it may hang after the storage is safe.
- Not yet shown: Bluetooth pairing/OBEX on this root, a USB-C link, and
  multi-core (205).
- The host link needs CJ's `deviceinterfaced` kill after almost every reboot.
