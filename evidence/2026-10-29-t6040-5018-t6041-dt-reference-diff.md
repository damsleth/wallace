## Ranked J614s additions

1. **Internal DCP and panel backlight:** map J614s ADT DCP, DART, display, clock, PMGR and PMP resources and panel properties; check installed firmware version against 26.6 driver ABI, loader reservations and driver dependencies. Establish ownership and safe brightness limits before enabling. Gravity's disabled DCP node is a reference, not a ready patch.
2. **AOP/ADMAC and board audio:** identify J614s AOP, DMA, DART, I²C and amp wiring and obtain compatible firmware and drivers. Derive speaker slots, gain, thermal model and protection from J614s before enabling output; microphone capture also needs matching AOP ABI.
3. **PMP reporting and required PMGR domains:** establish J614s report IDs, parent chains, resident firmware, calibration handoff and power ownership before integrating the display or ISP dependencies. Confirm that this path is actually needed for J614s.
4. **ISP/camera:** map J614s ISP and DART resources, power dependencies, sensor, firmware, calibration and privacy LED before preparing a board node. Do not reuse the Max setfile.

## Unknowns

- J614s internal display and audio board topology, exact interrupt/domain assignments and raw-to-CPU translations remain to be sourced from its own ADT; J773s establishes same-SoC resources only.
- The installed J614s firmware/stub version, DCP 26.6 compatibility, PMP/AOP firmware and driver ABI combinations remain unverified. DCP callback presence does not prove working brightness or VRR.
- J614s ISP sensor and calibration, GPU variant and firmware stack, and PMP/PMGR runtime ownership remain unverified. The Gravity fragment's disabled states and experimental comments are not validation.
- No hardware, network, kernel build, commit or push was performed. No T6041 address is authorized for T6040 access by this comparison.
