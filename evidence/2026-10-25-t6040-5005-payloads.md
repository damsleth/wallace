## Revision 2026-10-25b: SD64 is unavailable, so the root is host-built

CJ could not find the SD64 card. A 2 TB FAT32 card (`SD2TB`) is in the slot,
and nothing touches it. Changes:

- **Root image** (`scripts/t6040-build-nvmeroot-image.sh`): built from the
  pinned Alpine 3.24 dwm/i3 desktop RAM root `initramfs-alpine-dwm-i3-v5`
  (`25d4b110`), which has Xorg, i3, dwm, st, wpa_supplicant, iw, bluetoothd,
  dbus and udevd. It gets a persistent-root profile:
  - OpenRC sysinit `devfs dmesg procfs sysfs`, boot `hostname bootmisc
    t6040-keymap`, default `dbus bluetooth`, shutdown `killprocs mount-ro`.
  - Norwegian console keymap (pinned bmap + service + `conf.d/keymaps`),
    Xorg `XkbLayout no`, `nb_NO` locale.
  - The RAM-root `t6040-data-mount`/`t6040-data-sync` are **removed**: they
    poll and mount every NVMe partition read-write.

  The image is ext4, 16 GiB, UUID `988d97b4`, label `wallace-root`, built with
  `mkfs.ext4 -d` in a throwaway Alpine container. `e2fsck -fn` is clean. Raw
  sha256 `e3fa66b5…`, `wallace-root.img.xz` `ab73d13f…` (27 MB). Not
  byte-reproducible (mkfs timestamps); the artifact is pinned.
- **Installer B** (`d2c564f0`, a == b): carries the `.xz` and its raw hash.
  `xzcat` writes to p3 and hashes in-stream against the pin; caches are then
  dropped, the file re-hashed from the SSD, and checked with `e2fsck -fn`

  re-UUID or resize. `tune2fs` and `resize2fs` are removed and asserted
  absent. Write-through, the dirty-p3 repair, all gates, writes == p3, and the
  clean `reboot -f` are kept.

  - Scaled install (1 GiB test image, 4 KiB-sector exFAT loop): PASS, and a
    second run refuses.
- **Step A** becomes `payload-A-nvmeread`: the new kernel+DTB with the 6001
  read-only NVMe tester initramfs `524d94c3` and its checker. The v5 RAM root
  cannot be the first boot, because its data-mount mounts p3 read-write.
