- At `80badc91`, [main.c:229](/Users/damsleth/Code/m1n1/src/main.c:229) calls `nvme_shutdown()` unconditionally before vectoring. Its uninitialized path performs full NVMe init/adoption, then shutdown/reset. **The effective safeguard is this path, not merely the presence of `nvme_ensure_shutdown()`.** Some early init failures return without resetting ANS; quiescence is therefore not guaranteed on every failure.
- I found no first-stage-versus-chainload gate bypassing embedded payload handling, PCIe initialization, or WiFi/BT DT fixups. However, identical embedded DTB bytes do **not** imply identical final DT: firmware/ADT inputs, reservations and framebuffer state affect fixups.

It does **not** reproduce pristine iBoot ANS state: rollback m1n1 has already initialized hardware and executed its shutdown path. Chainload also relocates SEPFW/BootArgs and modifies ADT memory-map entries.
- Consequently, it does not establish startup-picker enrollment, the new volume’s firmware/boot-policy context, cable-free operation, or clean reboot back into the selected entry. Require a separate direct enrolled boot and clean reboot/filesystem check before declaring untethered success.
