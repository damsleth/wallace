# #asahi-dev review, 2026-07-31 → 08-03: two findings that change our blockers

Read for T6040 relevance. Two items are directly load-bearing for our open blockers, one is a
collaboration opportunity where **we are ahead of upstream**, and several are smaller corrections.

## 1. ⭐ Our SMP bug (205) is a KNOWN M4 issue — and it is CONFIG-DEPENDENT

2026-08-02, the single most valuable exchange in four days of logs:

> `10:02 <sven>` yuka: does linux boot without the weird crashes with smp on 26.x or 27 now?
> `10:43 <yuka>` I haven't seen the "trying to write to read-only [...]" since a few weeks now, but
> **it depends very much on the kernel config used**. I guess I would need to try some kernel with
> fedora config to make a definite statement

Three things follow:

1. **"the weird crashes with smp"** is an established, named problem among the M4 developers — not
   something unique to our port. Ticket 205 is not a Wallace bug.
2. **"trying to write to read-only"** is *verbatim* our original signature (`Unable to handle kernel
   write to read-only memory`, the `unpack_to_rootfs` fault recorded in 121 and in the
   `smp-maxcpus6-unpack-rootfs-fault` memory).
3. **"depends very much on the kernel config used"** independently corroborates our round-7 result:
   `rodata=on` (block-mapped linear map) took maxcpus=5 from *total hang* to *complete boot with 5
   CPUs and no panic*. Config sensitivity is exactly what yuka reports, and `RODATA_FULL` is a
   config knob.

**We are not stuck, we are converging with upstream.** And our contribution is concrete: nobody in
the logs has isolated *which* config knob matters, and we have a three-line reproducer plus a
specific knob (`rodata=full` vs `on`) that changes the failure qualitatively.

Actions: ticket **215** (diff our config against yuka's / a Fedora config, and build-time
`CONFIG_RODATA_FULL_DEFAULT_ENABLED=n`), and a draft question for CJ to put to yuka asking which
config changes made it go away.

## 2. ⭐ We are AHEAD of upstream on T6040 PCIe — yuka is hitting the symptom we already fixed

2026-08-01:

> `23:09 <yuka>` T8132/T6040 pcie is super annoying. On T8132 the ethernet no longer wants to show up
> at all, while on **T6040 it appears followed by "link down"** and failed to change from D3hot to
> D0? I still didn't try if port 0 actually works for wifi.

"Appears, then link down" is **precisely** the failure we root-caused on 2026-07-29: the endpoints
are never powered, because their power-enable lines are **SMC key writes**, not AP GPIOs —
`/amfm function-reg_on = pKW4('gP13', …)` → `pwren-gpios = <&smc_gpio 0x13 …>` on port00, and
`gP19` → line 25 on port01 (the SD reader). With those two `pwren-gpios` in place our WiFi, BT and
SD reader all came up and have been working since.

yuka explicitly has not tried port 0 for WiFi yet. **This is the one place where our port is clearly
ahead**, and it is a small, self-contained fix that would unblock her. Drafted for CJ (agents do not
post) — ticket **216**.

## 3. NVMe: upstream's own reading matches ours, plus the preferred design

2026-08-02, 14:04-15:15 — directly relevant to 192/206:

> `<yuka>` The comment on `APPLE_ANS_UNKNOWN_CTRL` in the kernel driver is very confusing, and does
> not match what I can observe anywhere. Everything points to 15.x+ fw simply **missing**
> `APPLE_ANS_UNKNOWN_CTRL` and behaving as 14.x without clearing `APPLE_ANS_PRP_NULL_CHECK`
> `<yuka>` since we changed `cwd12 = 1 -> cwd12 = 0`, `nvme_read()` works fine without
> `APPLE_ANS_PRP_NULL_CHECK` on old and new firmware
> `<sven>` `NULL_PRP_PTR_ERR F2H … ` ← pretty sure that's the error message i saw without that bit
> `<sven>` just introduce a new compatible, add `apple,firmware-abi` … and then just change behaviour
> for macos 15+

This corroborates the gate we already ship (skipping the legacy `LINEAR_SQ_CTRL`/`UNKNOWN_CTRL`
writes on our `mBoot-18000` firmware) and — importantly — gives the **upstream-preferred shape**:
a new `compatible` plus an `apple,firmware-abi` property, *not* firmware-version sniffing. That
directly supersedes the `V_UNKNOWN` gate discussion in our drafts: when this is eventually offered
upstream it should be an ABI property, not a version comparison. Recorded in ticket 206.

Note also `sven` at 15:08: "uh, also cwd12 isn't prp" — a caution that yuka's `cdw12` change and the
PRP-null-check are separate things. Do not conflate them.

## 5. Smaller items worth knowing

- **m1n1 ATC PHY tunable bugs** (`chaos_princess`, 07-31): `kboot_atc.c:170` — `AUS_VREG_TRIM` has a
  length inconsistent with every other instance (5); and `kboot_atc.c:74` applies `USB_ACIOPHY_TOP`
  where it should be USB-case-only, with `CIO_ACIOPHY_TOP` being the TB case. If our USB work ever
  resumes, these are real upstream bugs in the tunable application path we depend on.
- **`firmware_class.path` may accept a list** (`"$FIRST\0$SECOND"`, jannau 07-31) and there is an
  active thread about making it take multiple paths. Relevant to our 214 finding that firmware
  requested during probe **must** live in the initramfs: a multi-path search would let firmware live
  on the real root instead. Worth tracking, not worth relying on.
- **`cpu_features->apple_sysregs_unlocked`** (yuka, 08-01) is the canonical way to test whether
  impdef sysregs are locked in raw boot mode, exposed in proxyclient as
  `u.cpu_features.apple_sysregs_unlocked`. Cleaner than our ad-hoc checks.
- **sven wants a live ADT captured while macOS is running** (07-31 21:51, again 08-01 05:34): "if
  someone can get me a live ADT from apple entitled boot mode on a machine with SPTM and TXM that
  would be great". CJ has exactly such a machine (M4 Pro, macOS 26.x, SPTM). `mmediouni` suggests
  `ioreg -l` covers most of it. Low-effort, high-goodwill contribution — ticket **217**.
- m1n1 PRs worth watching: 644 (apfs/gigalocker build fix), 645 (t6030 isp pmgr offset), 648 (t8103
  26.6 fw test), 649 (publish m1n1.bin to CDN per tag).
