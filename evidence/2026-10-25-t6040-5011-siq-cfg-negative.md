# 5011 (claude, 2026-10-25): symmetric SIQ_CFG_EL1 = 2 does NOT fix fault 205

Review: Mythos PASS with must-fixes (`evidence/2026-10-25-review-5011-siq-cfg.md`),
all applied. CJ approved. Lease `claude`.

## Variants

m1n1 `80badc91` plus `patches/m1n1-t6040-siq-cfg.patch`, built in a clean
worktree with one toolchain:
`PATH=$HOME/.cargo/bin:$PATH RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN=nightly make -j8 EXTRA_CFLAGS="-Wstack-usage=2048 [-DT6040_SIQ_CFG]" M1N1_VERSION_TAG=v1.6.0-wallace-80badc91`.
- **siq** `7cbf67fe…` (`.siq_cfg = true` in features_m4) and **control**
  `bf4b2462…` differ in exactly one byte, `features_m4.siq_cfg`.
- **Verify** (5010 script over siq, reads only): `IMP_APL_SIQ_CFG_EL1 = 0x2` on
  cpu0, cpu1 and cpu5. Under 7de07c71 it was 0 / 3 / 3.

Payload: ticket 217's storage-free artifact (daily301 Image `a9a2096f`, thin
dcuart DTB `30b01736`, initramfs-cow-report `de1a5cd7`).

## Result (fresh boot each, maxcpus=6)

| boot | console | CPUs up | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| control 6a | `6c49f10d…` | 6 | **Oops at 0.0228 s on CPU 5**, ESR 0x9600004f (EL1 write, L3 permission fault), `__pi_memcpy_generic` ← `copy_folio_from_iter_atomic` (async initramfs unpack), PTE `00e8010004618707` (writable). Initramfs unusable, reproducer never ran. |
| **siq 6a** | `64e8692f…` | 6 | **Same: Oops at 0.0214 s on CPU 5**, same signature and path, PTE `00e8010005b9c707` (writable). |

Mythos's stop rule ("siq faults at all, then negative, stop") applies. The remaining
six boots were cancelled.

## Conclusions

- Making SIQ_CFG_EL1 symmetric (2 on every core, as m1n1 does on M1–M3) does
  not change the fault. SIQ_CFG is **not** the 205 cause. Variant B (writing
  0 on the secondaries) is not worth running: the fault is unchanged with
  cpu0 itself moved off 0, too.
- A new, faster harness: at maxcpus=6 on the thin DTB with the 217 payload,
  the fault is **deterministic in 2 of 2 boots, on CPU 5, about 21 ms after
  start**, in the initramfs unpack. One boot gives a yes/no result in
  seconds (the launcher's 480 s wait for a report that never comes can be
  short-circuited when an Oops lands before the first `RUN_BEGIN`). This is
  the same signature as the July `smp-maxcpus14` log and the
  "maxcpus>=6 unpack_to_rootfs" memory.
- Next (Mythos P2): a diagnostic in the fault path. On an EL1 write
  permission fault at a linear-map VA whose PTE is writable, do a local
  `tlbi`, then `dsb`/`isb`, retry once and log the result. That separates
  stale-TLB from not-TLB on the real event, and this harness can answer it
  in one boot.
